Our signature journey

Garden to Old Town

จากสวนสู่เมืองเก่า

Start in a century-old lychee orchard and step off two hours later at Wat Arun. This is the way Bangkok used to travel — out through our own canal, past stilt houses and neighbourhood temples, to the Giant Buddha at Wat Paknam, and on into the old city. We don't bring you back; we deliver you where you actually want to be.

Choose your 30-minute stop

Option one

Wat Paknam

Climb the white chedi for the glass stupa and the view back over the canals you just travelled.

Option two

Artist House

A 200-year-old wooden house on the canal, with traditional puppet shows and coffee on the deck.

Choose where you finish

Option one

Wat Arun

Step off at the Temple of Dawn and walk straight in, with Wat Pho and the Grand Palace a short ferry across the river.

Option two

Rajinee Pier

200m to MRT Sanam Chai, 400m to the flower market, and a one-minute ferry to Wat Arun if you change your mind.

Departures

10:00 – 12:00 12:00 – 14:00 14:00 – 16:00 16:30 – 18:30 Sunset

Four departures daily. The sunset run is the one that books out first. In high water the canal lock can close, so we confirm the final route the day before.
